# Sources and evidence register

Dates below identify publication, updates, or the observation period. The shared evidence review date is recorded in the [methodology](METHOD.md). “Living documentation” means a reliable original publication date was not established; the version reviewed is the evidence.

There are **45 source records**. Some records contain a closely related supporting page, so this is not a claim of 45 independent studies. Platform documentation establishes stated functionality and rules, not independent proof of business performance. Original observational studies and a preprint are identified separately. No third-party articles or datasets are reproduced in full.

## S01

**Statcounter — Search Engine Market Share Worldwide.** [Source](https://gs.statcounter.com/search-engine-market-share). Observation: October 2026. Type: original measurement provider. Use: global referral distribution. Limitation: participating-site referrals; not search-query share or a comprehensive assistant-usage measure. Dynamic page can change.

## S02

**Statcounter — FAQ.** [Source](https://gs.statcounter.com/faq). Living methodology. Use: definition of search referrals and sampling. Limitation: sections may have different update dates; historical illustrative sample counts were not treated as current panel sizes. Published data carries Statcounter's stated attribution/share-alike terms; see its page for details.

## S03

**Statcounter — Search Engine Market Share India.** [Source](https://gs.statcounter.com/search-engine-market-share/all/india). Observation: October 2026. Type: original measurement provider. Use: India-specific distribution. Limitation: same referral constraints as S02. The exact country path matters; the ambiguous `/india/` path was rejected as a country-data source.
